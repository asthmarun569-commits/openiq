# OpenIQ pilot protocol checklist

This is a planning aid, not ethics approval or a substitute for qualified research support.

1. **Define the construct and intended population.** Explain what each domain is meant to measure and why each item belongs there.
2. **Review the bank.** Independently check answer keys, clarity, language/cultural loading, accessibility and item-to-construct mapping. Conduct cognitive interviews.
3. **Consent and privacy.** Obtain voluntary informed consent, minimize data collection, use pseudonymous participant IDs, store consent separately, restrict access and set retention/deletion rules. Follow applicable ethics and privacy requirements.
4. **Freeze and version the test.** Record item-bank and site versions for every response set.
5. **Plan the sample and analyses.** Sample size should be determined based on intended precision and model by a statistician/psychometrician, not by a universal rule of thumb.
6. **Inspect items.** Review proportion correct, missingness, response-option patterns where available, corrected item-total correlations and floor/ceiling effects. Do not delete items based on one statistic alone.
7. **Reliability.** Choose reliability estimates appropriate to the intended score. KR-20/alpha alone do not establish validity or unidimensionality.
8. **Validity and fairness.** Gather evidence for content, response processes, internal structure, external relationships and accessibility/fairness. These require appropriate design and data.
9. **Norms only if justified.** Percentiles or age-standardized scores require appropriate representative norming and a defensible scoring model.
10. **Independent review.** Publish methods, sample description, results, uncertainty, exclusions, item changes and limitations. Do not describe OpenIQ as a validated IQ test without adequate evidence and independent review.
