OpenIQ.Quality and validation status · v0.11

TECHNICAL AND PSYCHOMETRIC AUDIT

Eight essential elements

This page separates fixes implemented in the software from evidence that must be established through research. OpenIQ remains an experimental reasoning quiz, not a validated IQ test.

Current decision: not ready for IQ-score reporting

Norm-referenced scoring is disabled. No IQ accuracy percentage, FSIQ, or validated cognitive-index score is claimed.

1. Scientific test design

Status: partial / development required. Items have stable parameter-based IDs and answer keys. However, the current procedural item bank is not a validated representation of cognitive ability. Internal codes are retained for research-data continuity, but the test UI now displays neutral practice-category names. These categories are not validated cognitive indexes.

Rectification: keep raw practice scoring; conduct expert review and cognitive interviews; document each item, intended construct, scoring rule, and revision history.

2. Psychometric validation

Status: not established. The Research Lab offers exploratory item difficulty, Wilson intervals, corrected item-total correlations, and guarded KR-20 estimates. Software tests do not establish reliability or validity.

Rectification: use a preregistered protocol, fixed/calibrated forms, an adequate sample, reliability and validity studies, and independent analysis.

3. Norms and scoring

Status: not established; score conversion disabled. A raw percentage is not an IQ score. Applying a mean-100/SD-15 formula without appropriate norms does not create valid IQ measurement.

Rectification: do not enable norm conversion until suitable age-specific norms, scoring evidence, uncertainty estimates, and independent review exist for the exact test version.

4. Test delivery

Status: basic delivery with limitations. The site runs in a browser. Timed WMI stimuli now use a presentation phase, including the mental-update task; PSI symbol stimuli remain visible without the WMI timed-hide rule. Response timing starts when answer choices appear and resets when a question is rendered.

Rectification: test on multiple phones, browsers, and input methods. Device-dependent timing means PSI must not be interpreted as standardized processing speed yet.

5. Results reporting

Status: safer experimental reporting. Results are raw correct counts and percentages, with a clear non-IQ warning. Exports include app, generator, scoring, form, and item presentation metadata.

Rectification: publish only score interpretations supported by evidence; eventually add validated uncertainty estimates and documented score interpretation.

6. Security and privacy

Status: local-only data handling, with limits. The static site does not automatically upload answers. Export files contain item-level answers and can be identifying in combination. Client-side answer keys are inspectable and cannot be treated as secret.

Rectification: minimize data, keep participant files out of the public repository, define retention/deletion and access controls, and obtain appropriate consent and ethics review before research recruitment.

7. Ethics and fairness

Status: basic consent language, formal requirements unresolved. Research participation is optional and separate from taking the quiz. The research pilot is intended for adults, but self-attested consent alone does not replace required ethics review.

Rectification: confirm applicable institutional and local requirements, document recruitment and withdrawal procedures, check accessibility, and evaluate item functioning and score comparability across intended groups.

8. Quality assurance and maintenance

Status: basic automated tests. Regression tests cover generation, answer keys, consent/export fields, and scoring safeguards. These tests verify software behavior only.

Rectification: version every release, preserve a frozen validation build, add browser/device testing, review changes, and rerun tests after every update.

Release gates

  1. Complete item review and fixed-form calibration.
  2. Resolve test-delivery and response-timing issues.
  3. Run a consented exploratory pilot; report uncertainty and limitations.
  4. Establish reliability, validity, and form comparability.
  5. Collect appropriate age norms and estimate measurement error.
  6. Conduct independent reference-test validation before any IQ claim.

95% target: not established. If defined as 95% of scores within ±5 points of an appropriate independent reference score, test it on a preregistered independent sample and report a confidence interval, bias, MAE, RMSE, and subgroup results.
