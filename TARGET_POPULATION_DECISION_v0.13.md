# Required decision before norming

The intended norm population has not been specified sufficiently to recruit or interpret norms. Before any norming study, the project owner and psychometric lead must document:

- intended age range and whether separate age bands are required;
- geographic coverage and intended language(s);
- educational and literacy assumptions;
- intended use (practice, research, screening, or other) and prohibited uses;
- accessibility accommodations and how they affect comparability;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria and recruitment/sampling frame;
- the independent reference assessment and who is qualified/permitted to administer it;
- ethics review, informed consent, privacy, retention and deletion rules.

Do not collect norming data until these decisions and applicable research review are complete. Do not use names, phone numbers, exact birth dates, or other direct identifiers in analysis exports. Keep any re-identification key separately under controlled access.
