# OpenIQ v0.13.1 Evidence Study Protocol

## Status
This is a protocol and analysis scaffold, not evidence that OpenIQ is reliable, valid, fair, or normed. Artificial rows are software demonstrations only. Existing exports lack suitable independent reference scores and a documented representative sampling frame.

## Freeze before study
Specify construct, intended interpretation/use, age range, languages, region, administration mode, exact build/form/scoring/timing rules, exclusions, independent reference instrument, qualified administrator requirements, primary outcomes, missing-data rules, sample-size method, subgroup analyses, and stopping rule. Do not call output IQ unless qualified experts support that interpretation.

## Reliability and precision
Use a prespecified test–retest design with frozen form, justified interval and controlled conditions; report appropriate ICC, confidence intervals, mean change and practice effects. Alternate forms require evidence of comparability; correlation alone is insufficient. Evaluate internal consistency only where the construct model justifies it; alpha/KR-20 alone does not establish unidimensionality. Report SEM/conditional precision where justified. Record interruptions, visibility changes, device and actual elapsed time. Fix timing/navigation defects before recruitment.

## Validity
Content: independent qualified reviewers map items to the blueprint and document relevance, representativeness, ambiguity, reading load, cultural assumptions, accessibility and answer-key checks. Response process: cognitive interviews across the target population. Internal structure: prespecified analyses appropriate to the construct and sample. Relations to other variables: use a suitable independent assessment for the same construct, age/language group and interpretation, administered/scored under authorized standard procedures by qualified personnel. Consequences: assess likely misclassification and misuse. Report bias, MAE, RMSE, calibration and difference/agreement plots; correlation is not agreement.

## Independent comparison and ±5 target
Keep development/calibration separate from held-out validation. Where comparable IQ-point scores are legitimately available, preregister absolute error <=5, the proportion meeting that criterion and its 95% CI. Proposed decision rule: lower confidence bound >=0.95, subject to statistician review. Also report signed bias, MAE, RMSE, score-range behavior, missingness and subgroup uncertainty. A statistician/psychometrician must calculate sample size from the chosen CI method and desired precision/power before recruitment. A software minimum is not a sample-size justification. Never substitute web quizzes, self-reported IQ, marks or simulated values for an independent reference assessment.

## Fairness and accessibility
Plan language/proficiency and schooling language; education and literacy/numeracy exposure; disability and accommodations; device/browser/screen/input method and timing; culture/region and unfamiliar contexts. Conduct cognitive interviews and translation/adaptation review. Preregister subgroup analyses; report effect sizes and uncertainty. Small groups are inconclusive, not proof of fairness. Use DIF methods only where sample size and method support them. Do not infer ability from disability.

## Sampling, privacy and ethics
Define the target population before sampling. Website convenience samples cannot establish population-wide norms. Document sampling frame, stratification/weighting, nonresponse, age bands and uncertainty. Obtain informed consent, minimize data, set secure storage/retention/deletion rules, and determine required ethics review before collection. Do not include direct identifiers in analysis files.

## Gates
1. Reproduce/fix timer and regression-test it. 2. Expert content/key review. 3. Cognitive interviews/accessibility review. 4. Pilot and item calibration. 5. Reliability/precision study. 6. Norming for a precisely defined population. 7. Independent held-out validation. 8. External review and transparent technical report. Artificial data cannot pass any evidence gate.
