OpenIQ.Research pilot workflow

PILOT RESEARCH · V0.10

Participant information and data handling

Purpose

OpenIQ is an experimental reasoning quiz. This pilot explores usability and item performance. It is not a clinical assessment and does not provide a validated IQ score.

Before participation

  • Participation is voluntary. You may decline without penalty.
  • The quiz is intended for adults aged 18 or older in this pilot workflow.
  • Do not enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, exact birth dates, or other identifying details.
  • The GitHub Pages version runs in your browser. It does not automatically send answers to a research server.
  • If you choose to participate, your downloaded JSON contains a random participant code, selected age band if provided, answers, correctness, item metadata, and response times.
  • You decide whether and with whom to share that file. A participant code is pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity.
  • Do not use these results for diagnosis, hiring, school placement, or other consequential decisions.

Researcher checklist

  1. Obtain any required ethics review and informed consent before recruitment.
  2. Use a written protocol describing the research question, inclusion criteria, data access, retention period, and withdrawal process.
  3. Give each participant a unique code. Do not maintain a code-to-identity key unless necessary and approved; keep any such key separate and protected.
  4. Import JSON files into Research Lab only on a trusted device. The analysis is local to that browser tab.
  5. Keep raw data access restricted, delete it according to the approved retention schedule, and report aggregate findings.
  6. Document that question forms are randomized and may not be equivalent in difficulty.

Structured export fields

Each JSON export contains session metadata and a response-level array. Key fields include participantCode, ageBand, consent, itemId, templateId, difficulty, domainCode, correct, and responseTimeMs. Research consent is separate from agreement to take the quiz.
