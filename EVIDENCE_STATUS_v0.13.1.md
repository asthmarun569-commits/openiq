# OpenIQ evidence status — 2026-10-20

## Evidence currently available
Previously supplied JSON exports show prototype results only. They do not provide independent reference scores, a representative sampling frame, retest design, or documented demographic/accessibility sampling. They cannot establish reliability, validity, fairness, norms, or IQ-point agreement.

## Added in v0.13.1
- Protocol for reliability, validity, fairness, independent comparison and norming.
- Paired-reference, retest and fairness-review templates.
- Guarded paired-score analysis script reporting bias, MAE, RMSE, within-±5 proportion and bootstrap interval.
- Artificial dataset explicitly marked ineligible and rejected by the script.

## Not established
No real participants recruited; no independent reference assessment administered; no real reliability, validity, fairness or norm result; no evidence supporting 95% within ±5; no live website change or deployment.
