Item statistics and uncertainty

Difficulty = proportion correct. The interval shown is a 95% Wilson interval for the item difficulty proportion; it can be wide for small samples. Corrected item-total correlation is calculated against the remaining score within the same domain among respondents who saw that exact item. It is a rough discrimination indicator, not a validity result.

Domain summaries

KR-20 assumes dichotomously scored items and is only an internal-consistency estimate. SEM is a model-based raw-score precision estimate, not an IQ-point error bound. Neither establishes test validity, temporal stability, fairness, or accuracy of IQ estimates. A value from this convenience sample is not a norm.
